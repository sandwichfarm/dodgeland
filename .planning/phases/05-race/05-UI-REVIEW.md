The contract preserves actual-ground fog exposure and targeting versus monotonic
earned-frontier boundary movement. Fatal settlement precedes finish; living
same-tick finishes precede timeout. Innate 100-block and personal 250-tile recovery
remain distinct. Twelve-active-second reclaim preserves existing deadlines and
does not grant protection. All-down offers use the active Race clock, while
all-controller waiting suspends it.

Race-only Mud Splash/Fog Lantern offers, ineligible existing cards/packs/common
Revival/Flare, one owner-labelled market/hand, immutable ties, and unranked DNF
groups match the resolved context. The contract requires no-scroll responsive
layouts, both themes, reduced motion, and muted real-built browser evidence.

This is design approval, not completion of implementation or visual proofs.
